# Product Context — why this exists

The **trust identity is the product**: an honest field companion. Three rules follow from it
and are non-negotiable in every future change.

- **Never fabricate.** Absent data renders `--` or an empty state — in telemetry, regulations
  and stats alike. (The one violation ever shipped was a Gaussian "active fish" count; it was
  deleted and is listed under "do not resurrect" in `docs/CONTRACT.md`.)
- **A spot is never public.** GPS, reach and tackle stay private in every tier; the public
  Brag Board is deliberately location-free.
- **Offline is a first-class state.** A catch logged in a dead zone is never lost.

The retention loop is **trust + a private season**, not a leaderboard — public leaderboards
reward posting, and posting-at-scale rewards lying.
